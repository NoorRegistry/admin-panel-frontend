# Login carousel illustrations

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using the approved Option A login mockup as the style reference. These transparent PNGs are consumed by the login feature carousel through Next.js Image optimization.

- `public/images/login/orders.png`: parcel, delivery van and checkmark.
- `public/images/login/catalogue.png`: bodysuit, feeding cup and product tag.
- `public/images/login/inventory.png`: stock boxes and approval clipboard.

## Prompt set

Shared instruction: Create a new standalone transparent illustration asset using only the soft 3D visual style of the approved mockup's left illustration. Do not reproduce the web page. Use cream #FFF5E9, terracotta #CF8169 and deep terracotta #935C4B; refined matte ceramic/paper materials, subtle ambient lighting and clean silhouettes. Center the complete composition in a landscape 4:3 canvas with generous transparent margins. Genuine alpha transparency around and between objects; no background, floor, backplate, gradient, waves, text, numbers, wordmark or login UI. Intended to sit on a terracotta gradient in the desktop login feature carousel.

Orders subject: One cream shipping gift parcel with terracotta ribbon at left, a small matte terracotta delivery van at lower right, and a floating cream circular checkmark at upper right, connected by one elegant cream curved route.

Catalogue subject: A coordinated cluster of a cream baby bodysuit on a terracotta hanger, a terracotta feeding cup and a cream rectangular product tag with a small embossed terracotta plus. Physical matte objects, not UI cards; one balanced still life.

Inventory subject: Three cream and pale terracotta shipping boxes at different heights, a small cream clipboard with three embossed terracotta checkmarks and a small terracotta notification circle; one balanced matte sculpture still life.
